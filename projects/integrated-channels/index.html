Integrated Channels Suite

Digital Banking Platform

Senior Product Manager Dec 2024 – Mar 2026 FinTech Enterprise Platform
01 · Overview

Building a scalable digital banking platform

Integrated Channels Suite was a multi-module banking platform designed to bring multiple enterprise applications together through configurable workflows, secure integrations and scalable deployment capabilities.

Platform Type Digital Banking
Applications 12+ enterprise applications
Architecture API-first
Focus Integration & workflow automation
02 · Business Challenge

Simplifying fragmented enterprise operations

Enterprise banking operations can involve multiple applications, workflows and integrations. The product initiative focused on simplifying these fragmented operations through a scalable platform approach.

The product needed to support secure integrations, configurable workflows, transaction processing and scalable deployments across enterprise environments.

03 · Product Objective

Create a modular and scalable platform ecosystem

The objective was to define a platform architecture and product capabilities that could support multiple banking applications while improving interoperability and reducing integration complexity.
